# Dragon Bestiary — Part II

## Species 004 — Mosshorn Drake

**Scientific Name** — Sylvadraco muscicornis

**Ecological Class** — Forest Dweller

**Guild Profession(s)**

- Forestry
- Agriculture
- Reforestation
- Herbal Gathering

**Habitat** — Ancient forests, overgrown valleys, moss-covered cliffs, and humid woodlands.

**Diet** — Moss, ferns, berries, mushrooms, bark, medicinal herbs, and flowering shrubs.

**Temperament** — Gentle.

Observant.

Patient.

Highly territorial toward damaged forests but rarely aggressive toward people.

**Primary Instinct** — Mosshorns instinctively cultivate healthy forests.

As they travel, spores and seeds cling to the moss growing across their horns and scales. Over time they unknowingly spread vegetation across damaged woodland.

They are not planting forests intentionally.

They simply carry the forest with them.

Guild Discovery Lumber guilds noticed that abandoned logging sites visited by Mosshorns recovered far faster than untouched areas.

Modern forestry guilds often send Mosshorns into harvested regions before planting new saplings.

**Guild Applications**

- Reforestation
- Seed dispersal
- Medicinal herb discovery
- Sustainable logging
- Forest surveys

**Strengths**

- Excellent memory of forest trails.
- Naturally encourages plant growth.
- Rarely startles wildlife.
- Finds medicinal plants with remarkable consistency.

**Weaknesses**

- Stressed by barren environments.
- Performs poorly in cities.
- Avoids open grasslands.

**Care Difficulty** — Moderate.

Mosshorns require access to living vegetation and benefit greatly from quiet environments.

**Daily Routine** — Morning begins with slow wandering through woodland paths.

Afternoons are often spent grazing and spreading spores before resting beneath large trees during the evening.

**Interesting Behaviors** — Small birds frequently build temporary nests upon older Mosshorns.

The dragons appear completely unbothered.

**Relationship with Humans** — They trust calm workers who respect the forest.

Aggressive logging quickly destroys that trust.

**Relationship with Other Dragons** — Mosshorns often coexist peacefully with Riverveil Drakes.

Together they naturally maintain healthy riverbanks.

**Merchant Value** — High.

Healthy forests provide lumber, medicine, wildlife, and long-term sustainability.

**Species Story** — Legends claim the oldest forests remember every Mosshorn that has ever walked beneath their branches.

Whether truth or folklore, many foresters tell stories of forgotten woodland paths mysteriously reopening after a lone Mosshorn passed through the area. New flowers bloom where its footsteps once rested, and fallen trees gradually become homes for fresh life.

Perhaps the dragon is simply carrying seeds.

Or perhaps the forest itself quietly follows its oldest gardener.

Guildmaster's Observation "Never measure a Mosshorn's work by today's harvest. Measure it by what the forest becomes ten years from now."

**Evolution of Implementation** — Early

- Forest region

### Intermediate

- Tree regeneration

### Collections

- Plant database

### LINQ

- Search medicinal herbs

### Advanced

- Dynamic ecosystems

**Future Expansion**

- Seasonal forests
- Wildlife interactions
- Rare herbs
- Forest spirits (rumors only)
- Biodiversity mechanics

## Species 005 — Longtail Courier

**Scientific Name** — Aerodraco longicauda

**Ecological Class** — Grassland Dragon

**Guild Profession(s)**

- Courier
- Messenger
- Light Transport
- Exploration

**Habitat** — Rolling plains, open grasslands, coastal cliffs, and wide valleys.

**Diet** — Small game, insects, berries, and prairie grasses.

**Temperament** — Energetic.

Friendly.

Curious.

Impatient.

**Primary Instinct** — Longtail Couriers naturally roam enormous territories.

Unlike territorial dragons, they constantly patrol long distances in search of changing weather, migrating prey, and safe nesting grounds.

Guilds eventually realized these dragons could memorize trade routes with astonishing accuracy.

Guild Discovery Merchants observed that Longtails routinely traveled between distant settlements following nearly identical paths every season.

Eventually they began attaching lightweight satchels to cooperative dragons.

The first courier network was born.

**Guild Applications**

- Mail delivery
- Trade communication
- Small cargo
- Emergency dispatches
- Route scouting

**Strengths**

- Extremely fast.
- Exceptional memory.
- Easily trained.
- Excellent endurance.

**Weaknesses**

- Cannot carry heavy loads.
- Becomes restless if confined.
- Easily distracted by changing weather.

**Care Difficulty** — Low.

Longtails simply require room to run and frequent opportunities to travel.

**Daily Routine** — Morning patrols.

Long-distance courier routes.

Evening rest before beginning another journey at sunrise.

**Interesting Behaviors** — Every Longtail chooses one favorite resting location somewhere along its route.

Even after years away, it almost always returns there.

**Relationship with Humans** — Forms friendships quickly.

Often greets familiar workers before accepting deliveries.

**Relationship with Other Dragons** — Gets along well with nearly every species due to its naturally social disposition.

**Merchant Value** — Extremely High.

Fast communication transformed trade more than almost any other domesticated dragon.

**Species Story** — Before courier dragons existed, merchants often waited weeks for news from neighboring cities.

Then came the Longtails.

No one remembers who first trusted a dragon with an important letter, only that the dragon returned carrying another.

Within a generation, trade routes became lifelines connecting distant communities, and many historians argue that merchant guilds owe their existence as much to Longtail Couriers as they do to gold itself.

Guildmaster's Observation "Feed them before asking them to travel. Hungry Longtails have an unfortunate habit of accepting 'shortcuts' only they understand."

**Evolution of Implementation**

- Route system
- Mail mechanics
- Travel time
- Async deliveries
- World map expansion

**Future Expansion**

- Weather
- Reputation delivery
- Courier events
- Trade caravans
- Regional economies

Foundational Ecosystem The first five documented species represent the earliest partnerships between dragons and merchant guilds.

Together they support nearly every basic operation required for a successful settlement.

| Species | Natural Role | Guild Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Stonejaw Drake | Shapes mountains | Mining & Construction |
| Hearthscale Drake | Maintains warm nests | Smithing & Hospitality |
| Riverveil Drake | Protects waterways | Fishing & Water Management |
| Mosshorn Drake | Restores forests | Forestry & Agriculture |
| Longtail Courier | Roams vast territories | Communication & Transport |

Notice that no species exists solely to perform a job.

Each dragon evolved long before merchant guilds existed.

Guilds merely learned how to cooperate with instincts that nature had already perfected.

**Closing Thoughts** — This Bestiary is not a complete record of dragonkind.

It is merely the beginning.

Countless species remain undocumented beyond distant mountains, forgotten islands, dense jungles, frozen tundras, and unexplored caverns.

Some may become trusted companions.

Others may refuse human contact entirely.

Some may challenge everything scholars currently believe about dragons.

That uncertainty is intentional.

Discovery has always been one of the greatest rewards of exploration.

The Dragon Merchant Guild exists not to conquer nature, but to understand it.

And perhaps that is the greatest lesson every apprentice guildmaster must eventually learn.

**Guiding Principle** — A guild does not create great dragons.

> Great dragons shape great guilds.

May every new species added to this Bestiary honor that philosophy while adding another page to the ever- growing story of dragons and those fortunate enough to walk beside them.
