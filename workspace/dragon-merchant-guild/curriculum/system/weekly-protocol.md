# Weekly Protocol

## Friday block (replaces a normal day block, same 90–120 min)

1. **Clear ⚠ items** (40 min) — revisit every task marked ⚠ this week with fresh eyes. Still stuck? Formulate the question precisely and resolve it (docs, search); the act of writing the question precisely solves half of them.
2. **Run due reviews** (20 min) — everything due in `REVIEW-QUEUE.md`, plus one random old kata, cold.
3. **Groom the queue** (10 min) — bump stages on passes, reset fails to stage 1 and copy them to `weak-spots.md`.
4. **Retro** (10 min) — three lines at the bottom of the current module file: *what worked / what dragged / next week's risk*.
5. **Harvest** (10 min) — anything interview-worthy this week → `06-interview/question-bank.md` or `story-bank.md`. Milestone finished? → one bullet to `07-career/resume-bullets.md`.

## Advancement rule (checked every Friday)

- **≥80% of this week's day blocks ✅** → next week proceeds as scheduled.
- **<80%** → next week is a **catch-up week**: redo the unfinished blocks, no new content. This is a rule, not a judgment call — that's the whole point.
- Module days all done → TODAY pointer goes to the module's checkpoint (`checkpoints/cpN.md`), scheduled as its own session.

## Monthly (first Friday of the month, +15 min)

- Stage-3 review items that pass **graduate**: move them from REVIEW-QUEUE to `06-interview/question-bank.md` as answered questions.
- Any topic appearing twice in `weak-spots.md` → insert a dedicated remediation day block before the next checkpoint.
